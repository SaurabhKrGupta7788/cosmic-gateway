About ExoVision

An interactive journey into exoplanet discovery and machine learning.

ExoVision is more than just a website; it's an educational platform designed to bridge the gap between complex astronomical data and the fascinating world of artificial intelligence. Our mission is to provide a hands-on experience for enthusiasts, students, and researchers to explore how machine learning can be used to classify exoplanets from the vast dataset provided by the Kepler Space Telescope.

This project was born out of a passion for both space exploration and data science. We believe that by making these powerful tools accessible and interactive, we can inspire the next generation of scientists and developers.

Core Features

KOI Data Submission

Submit real or hypothetical Kepler Object of Interest (KOI) data. Our base model provides an instant classification, distinguishing between 'Confirmed' and 'Candidate' exoplanets based on the provided inputs.

Interactive Tuning

Step into the shoes of a machine learning engineer. Our hyperparameter tuning module allows you to visually construct and train your own neural network, offering a tangible understanding of how different parameters affect model behavior.

About the Developer

ExoVision is proudly developed by Coding Masters, a team passionate about the intersection of technology, data science, and space exploration. Our goal is to create intuitive and powerful tools that make complex scientific concepts accessible to everyone.
